Project

Ledgerline

A lightweight expense tracker with a CLI-first workflow, built because every app I tried wanted me to open a browser to log a coffee.

RoleDeveloper
StackPython, SQLite, CLI
Year2025
LinksSource ↗

Overview

Ledgerline is a command-line expense tracker. You log a purchase with one line in your terminal, and it handles categorizing, monthly totals, and a running budget check — no app to open, no account to log into.

The problem

I tried maybe six budgeting apps before building this. Every one of them added enough friction — opening the app, waiting for it to load, tapping through a few screens — that I'd stop logging small purchases within two weeks, which defeats the purpose of a budget tracker. The friction was the whole reason it failed, not the feature set.

The approach

The entire interface is a single command:

$ ledger add 4.50 coffee --cat food
$ ledger month
  Food        $132.40  ████████░░  66%
  Transport   $41.00   ███░░░░░░░  20%
  Other       $28.00   ██░░░░░░░░  14%

Everything is stored locally in SQLite. Categorization is mostly automatic based on past entries with the same description, and it only asks when it's genuinely unsure. The budget bars are ASCII on purpose — the constraint of staying in the terminal is what keeps it fast enough to actually use.

The result

I've logged expenses daily for over a year now, which is longer than I managed with any app before it. The next addition is a simple export to CSV for anyone who wants to hand the data to a spreadsheet or a real accountant at tax time.
