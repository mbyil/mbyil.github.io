F.01 — ABOUT

A little about how I got here.

I'm a software engineer by trade, currently focused on backend systems and developer tooling. Outside of work, I write about the technical problems I run into and the lessons that took longer than they should have to learn.

Photography started as a way to slow down. I mostly shoot landscapes and street scenes on trips, and I'm slowly working through the discipline of shooting less and looking more.

"I like building things that work, writing that's honest about the mess, and photos that don't need much explaining."
